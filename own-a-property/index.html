Long let or short let

Two ways to let the same property. Neither is right for everyone.

The right answer depends on the property, where it is, the season and the rules that apply there. Your report puts numbers to both for your address. Here is how they differ in practice.

AspectLong letShort let with a PropertyFlow Partner
IncomeOne rent, fixed for the term of the tenancy. Steady, and set once, so it does not move with demand.Many stays, priced night by night with AI Cognito Pricing. Income follows demand and the season, so busy months and quiet months both show up.
CostsThe tenant usually pays utilities and council tax. Letting agent fees, referencing and any void between tenancies sit with you.You pay utilities, broadband, cleaning, linen and consumables. What a PropertyFlow Verified Partner does

A local Partner in front. PropertyFlow behind them.

Your Partner introduces you and stays your local point of contact. The agreement, the licence, the client money and the operations sit with PropertyFlow. Here is what that means for you.

Your two contacts

A PropertyFlow Verified Partner in your area as your relationship contact, and a named Portfolio Manager in the back office for the day-to-day running of the property.

Licence and documents

PropertyFlow applies for and holds the short-term let licence or registration where the law requires one, and keeps the safety certificates and documents current with you.

Listing, pricing and guests

Your property is listed and priced across the booking channels with AI Cognito Pricing. Guests are messaged and looked after by the back office on the AI messenger, with your Partner looped in on escalations.

Cleaning and maintenance

Cleaning, changeovers, linen and maintenance are run through vetted Service Partners and Maintenance Partners, and charged to you at cost with no mark-up.

Your money

Every pound of booking money is held in a designated client account, separate from PropertyFlow's own money, and is never used for anyone else's property. You are paid monthly, on a single payout day, with an itemised statement showing every booking, every deduction and any tourist tax collected and paid on your behalf.

Fees, insurance and damage

The management fee is agreed per property before you sign and is stated in your Landlord Agreement, plus VAT where applicable. Licence and compliance costs are charged only with your approval. PropertyFlow holds Public Liability, Employer's Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance. Guest damage is handled under each booking channel's own damage protection. On Partner properties your Portfolio Manager files and manages the claim.

Tourist tax

Tourist tax, configured per council and calculated on each booking.

Some councils charge a tax on overnight stays. Where one applies to your property, PropertyFlow calculates it on every booking. The property owner or Partner remits it as the council requires.

Edinburgh

•5% of the accommodation cost
•Charged on the first five consecutive nights of a stay only
•2% of the tax collected is retained by providers for administration
•Remitted to the council as it requires

What PropertyFlow does

✓Calculates the tax on each booking, by council
✓Shows it on the guest's bill, under the council's official name where the council requires it
✓Itemises it on your monthly statement
✓On a Partner property, collects it and pays it on your behalf

Other councils

Each council that introduces a tourist tax sets its own rate and rules. PropertyFlow is configured per council, so when a new scheme starts the platform applies it to bookings in that area and your statement shows it.

Owner FAQs

Who contacts me after I send the form?

The PropertyFlow Verified Partner for your property's area. Every enquiry is routed by the address of the property, and the Partner gets in touch to talk through your report and what running the property would involve. If we do not yet cover the area, we tell you.

I live outside the UK. Can I still use this?

Yes. Overseas owners are included. Your Partner is local to the property, a named Portfolio Manager in the back office runs the day-to-day, and your itemised statement reaches you by email each month wherever you live. Include your country code with your phone number.

What is in the report, and how reliable is it?

We take the address, bedrooms, sleeps and standard you give us and set out what a long let and a short let might look like for that property, using market data from third-party providers, the ONS private rents index and our own platform data. This report is an estimate, not a valuation or a promise of returns, and you should not rely on it as one.

Who do I contract with, and what does it cost?

Your Partner introduces you and stays your local point of contact. The agreement, the licence, the client money and the operations sit with PropertyFlow. The management fee is agreed per property before you sign and is stated in your Landlord Agreement, plus VAT where applicable. Cleaning and maintenance are charged at cost with no mark-up, and licence and compliance costs only with your approval. The terms are summarised at Own a property: the terms in summary.

Does my property need a licence?

It depends where it is. Scotland requires a short-term let licence. Northern Ireland requires Tourism NI certification. Wales opens registration in October 2026, with a deadline of 31 March 2027. England has a registration scheme planned, and in London letting for more than 90 nights a year needs planning permission. On a Partner property PropertyFlow applies for and holds the licence or registration where the law requires one, and keeps your safety certificates current with you.

What if I want to stop, or go back to a long let?

Your Landlord Agreement sets out the notice period for ending the arrangement and what happens to bookings already made. Your Partner talks you through it before you sign.
