Getting set up

From application to your first introduction.

Seven steps, one named Portfolio Manager from day one. Here is how the first weeks go.

Step 1

Application

Tell us about yourself, your area and the landlords you could introduce. We reply within two working days.

Step 2

Call

A short call to check the fit both ways. The programme is by application and we accept around one in five.

Step 3

Agreement signed on DocuSeal

Accepted applicants receive the agreement and sign it electronically on DocuSeal.

Step 4

The Guide

The Partner Programme Guide arrives with your agreement. It answers the questions most applicants ask.

Step 5

Training

Your named Portfolio Manager walks you through the platform, how an introduction works and what a landlord receives.

Step 6

First introduction

You introduce your first landlord. PropertyFlow contracts with them, holds the licence and runs the property through its Service Partners. Guest messages are handled by the back office on the AI messenger, with you looped in on escalations.

Step 7

60-day ramp

Your named Portfolio Manager stays close while your first properties go live.

You trade under your own name and keep the relationship; PropertyFlow is the contracting party and licence holder for every property. Once accepted, you earn 50% of PropertyFlow's management fee, excluding VAT, on every property you introduce, paid monthly on a self-billed statement.
